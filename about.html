Why Nivora exists

Finding the right college, or the right job, usually happens through word of mouth, WhatsApp forwards and a lot of guesswork. Nivora Career Point was started to make that process a little more organised — one enquiry, one point of contact, one clear next step.

We work across two connected areas: helping students get into private colleges and universities, and helping job seekers and employers find the right fit in the private and IT sectors.

Where we work

We are based in Lakhasaria, Bihar, and currently serve students, job seekers and employers across:

  • Bihar
  • Jharkhand
  • Jaipur, Rajasthan
We only share verified information about institutions and job openings. If something hasn't been confirmed yet, we'll tell you that clearly rather than guess.
How we work

Principles we follow

No fake guarantees

We never promise guaranteed admission or guaranteed placement. We promise effort, follow-up and honest updates.

Verified information

Institution and job details are checked before they're shared with you, and we're upfront when something is still pending verification.

One person, one thread

Your enquiry is tracked from the first call through to the outcome — you're not repeating your story to a new person each time.
